A cat does not get one name and finish the matter. The name you call across the kitchen is only the first. There is another name that belongs to them among cats, and a third that stays slightly out of reach, which is as it should be.

Naming Buddy treats that idea, from T. S. Eliot's _The Naming of Cats_, as a small ceremony rather than a random list. You bring a photo and a sense of who they are. The app helps you leave with three names and a certificate you can keep.

## What the three names are for

- **The family name** is the everyday one. It has to sound right when you are half asleep, opening a tin, or asking them down from a cupboard.
- **The cat-world name** is theirs alone on Naming Buddy. No other cat on the platform carries it. That is the name that would look right on a tiny passport.
- **The ineffable name** is a poetic guess at the private one. You will never know if you have it exactly. The pleasure is in the near miss.

## How a ceremony runs

Start with a picture and a few true things about them. Independent, ridiculous, fond of the windowsill, terrible at sharing a chair. The app drafts a short portrait you can edit, then offers names that fit that portrait instead of a page of unrelated suggestions.

You confirm the three you want. The cat-world name is claimed so it stays unique. At the end you can download a keepsake certificate with the names on it.

If you already have a household nickname, keep it. The ceremony is happy to treat that nickname as the family name and spend its effort on the other two. New kittens, rescues, and cats who have been called "the kitten" for six months are all welcome.
