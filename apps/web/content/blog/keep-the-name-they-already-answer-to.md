A household nickname can stay. The ceremony spends its care on the other two.

If they already turn their head when you speak, that everyday name is doing its job. You do not have to retire it to give them a cat-world name of their own, or a poetic guess to set beside it.

Bring the nickname with you. The portrait and the other two names are where the ceremony puts its attention.
