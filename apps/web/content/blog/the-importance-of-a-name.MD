You call him Biscuit.

He pauses halfway through washing his paw. Slowly, he turns towards you.

Is that affection in his eyes? Recognition? Or the quiet disappointment of someone who considers himself a great deal more distinguished than a baked snack?

We cannot know, of course. But imagining the answer is part of the joy of living with cats.

In _The Naming of Cats_, T. S. Eliot offers a wonderfully feline idea: one name could never be enough. A cat needs an everyday name, a distinctive name worthy of its particular dignity, and a deeply private name known only to itself.

For our purposes, these are the **Family Name**, the **Cat-world Name**, and the **Ineffable Name**.

Together, they invite us to look a little more closely at the remarkable individual sleeping on our clean laundry.

### The Family Name: “This is where you belong”

The Family Name is the one woven into everyday life.

You call it when breakfast is ready. You whisper it when a nervous new arrival finally ventures out from beneath the sofa. You say it, with varying degrees of patience, when somebody is investigating your dinner.

It might be Gus, Luna, Jasper or, yes, Biscuit.

Whatever the choice, this name becomes part of your shared history. It gathers meaning through affection, familiarity and all those small moments that turn an animal in your house into a member of your family.

But what if, in our imagined feline world, your cat feels you have got it wrong?

Perhaps “Tiny” has grown into a magnificent creature who occupies most of the armchair. Perhaps “Angel” has developed a profitable sideline in knocking glasses off tables.

One imagines a formal complaint:

_“I appreciate the sentiment. However, I would like my name reviewed in light of my recent achievements.”_

Choosing thoughtfully means paying attention. Watch how your cat moves, plays, greets you and claims a favourite spot. The right name often emerges when you stop searching quite so hard and start noticing who is there.

### The Cat-world Name: “Do you know who I am?”

A Family Name establishes belonging. A Cat-world Name establishes standing.

At home, he may be Gus. Among the imagined feline aristocracy, he could be **Duke of Comfort**.

You see a cat sitting beside a radiator. He sees a sovereign surveying his winter estate.

This is the name that celebrates your cat’s particular presence: the fearless explorer, the velvet-footed diplomat, the affectionate household supervisor who attends every video call.

It need not sound royal. A small, watchful cat might suit **Keeper of the Garden Gate** far better than an elaborate title.

The point is distinction.

And if the name is insufficiently grand? Picture the indignity of arriving at a moonlit gathering as “Fluffy” while your neighbour introduces herself as “Countess of the Silver Orchard”.

There would be an ear twitch. Possibly two.

Creating a Cat-world Name gives us permission to celebrate qualities an everyday name cannot quite contain. It says: we have noticed your character, and it deserves recognition.

### The Ineffable Name: “Some things are mine alone”

Then comes the most mysterious name.

In Eliot’s poem, this is the cat’s private name: something humans cannot discover, however diligently they try.

Perhaps that explains those long periods of apparent contemplation.

We assume that our cat is staring at a wall. Perhaps a delicate matter of identity is under consideration.

_“Moonlight? Too obvious. The Feeling Before Dawn? Closer. But still…”_

In our playful imagining, even a cat might need time to settle upon something so personal. How do you capture an entire self in a name?

Perhaps the answer comes slowly, through sunlit mornings, midnight adventures and the quiet certainty of being loved.

Which raises a rather lovely question: **if we cannot know our cat’s Ineffable Name, could we help inspire it?**

### Helping your cat find its own answer

Taking Eliot’s idea into our own imaginative world, perhaps our role is to provide the experiences from which a private name might grow.

We can make room for our cats to express their particular character. Follow their invitation to play. Welcome their company when they seek it. Respect their retreat when they want solitude. Leave a little time simply to sit together, with no demand for a performance or a photograph.

A cat who investigates every cupboard offers us a different story from one who quietly keeps an elderly family member company. Both deserve to be noticed.

How we talk to them can become part of that attention.

“You’ve been very brave today.”

“There you are, my little keeper of the peace.”

“Have you come to watch the rain with me?”

These are affectionate human interpretations, of course. The idea that they might inspire a secret name belongs to our story. But speaking this way encourages us to recognise the small, distinctive things our cats do.

And perhaps, somewhere in that imagined feline mind, a phrase begins to take shape.

_“Keeper of the peace. Hmm. They may finally be getting somewhere.”_

### Could a suggested name offer a little inspiration?

This is where **Purrfectly Named** can join the adventure.

By sharing your cat’s photograph, personality, habits and backstory, you can explore names that reflect the individual you know. The richest clues may be small ones: how they greet you after a difficult day, where they go when the house falls quiet, or the surprising gentleness beneath their swagger.

For the Ineffable Name, think of the suggestions as **Ineffable Near-Names**: poetic possibilities that approach the mystery while leaving the secret with your cat.

A comfort-loving ginger cat might inspire **Warmth Keeper**. A quiet companion might suggest **Stillness Beside Me**. An adventurous soul could lead us towards **Beyond the Garden**.

You might try saying a favourite softly during a peaceful moment together. Let it become part of a little conversation, with no examination to pass and no answer required.

A purr, a glance or a flick of the tail makes a delightful ending to the scene, though it cannot confirm that you have uncovered a secret name.

Perhaps your suggestion is close. Perhaps it sends your cat’s private deliberations in an entirely new direction. Perhaps they consider it a promising first draft and spend the afternoon improving it.

Humans may submit suggestions. Final approval remains firmly with the cat.

### A name worth growing into

Choosing your cat’s names asks you to notice, appreciate and celebrate the companion sharing your life.

The Family Name welcomes them into your home. The Cat-world Name honours their individuality. The Ineffable Near-Name offers a little inspiration at the edge of something only they could know.

And the time you spend looking for those names has a value of its own. You may notice an overlooked habit, find a new way to describe their affection, or realise how much their quiet presence means to you.

Your cat may still ignore you when called.

But somewhere between your thoughtful suggestions and their next long nap, something rather wonderful might be taking shape, the creation of the ineffable.
